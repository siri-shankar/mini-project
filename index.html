ABOUT MEDIKIOSK

Healthcare history, made simpler.

Traditional patient history collection can be time-consuming and difficult to organise. MediKiosk aims to make this process easier through guided data collection and AI-assisted processing.

👨‍⚕️

For Patients

Provide your symptoms, medical history and previous medical documents through a simple interface.

🤖

AI Assistance

Organise patient-provided information into structured sections and generate a draft clinical summary.

🏥

For Doctors

Review organised patient information and AI-assisted summaries to support clinical history-taking.
